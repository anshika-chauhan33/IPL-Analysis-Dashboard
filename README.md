# 🏏 IPL Analytics Dashboard

An interactive data analysis dashboard designed to uncover patterns and historical trends across Indian Premier League (IPL) seasons. This project provides key insights into team performances, individual accolades, boundary distributions, and tactical variables such as toss decisions.

---

## 📌 Features & Visualizations

- **Season Champion & Team Branding:** Displays the tournament winner alongside dynamic team logos and season standings.
- **Top Individual Honors:**
  - 🟠 **Orange Cap:** Season top run-scorer with aggregate runs and strike rate.
  - 🟣 **Purple Cap:** Season leading wicket-taker with wickets and economy rates.
- **Boundary Distribution:** Visual analysis of boundary trends across seasons:
  - Total tournament 6s hit
  - Total tournament 4s hit
- **Toss Impact Analysis:**
  - **Match Win based on Toss Decision:** Visual breakdown of matches won by batting first vs. chasing after winning the toss.
  - **Toss Decision vs. Winning Percentage:** Correlation between choosing to field/bat and overall match conversion rates across different venues and seasons.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Analytics:** Tableau
- **Data Processing:** SQL
- **Dataset:** IPL Ball-by-Ball & Match-Level historical dataset

## 💡 Key Insights Uncovered

1. **Chasing Bias:** Teams electing to field first after winning the toss show a noticeable advantage, especially in day-night fixtures with dew factors.
2. **Boundary Inflation:** A marked upward trend in aggregate tournament sixes in recent seasons, illustrating modern T20 batting approaches.
3. **Orange & Purple Cap Correlation:** Analysis of whether having the top run-scorer or top wicket-taker directly correlates with winning the championship title.
